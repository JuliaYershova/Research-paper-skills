\section*{4\quad Results}
We predict hourly demand at 120 bike-sharing stations with a gradient-boosted
model and compare it with a seasonal baseline under five-fold cross-validation.

\begin{table}[h]

\caption{Mean absolute error (bikes per hour), lower is better.}

\midrule
Seasonal baseline & 4.82 & 120 \\
Boosted model     & 3.97 & 120 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
