\section*{4\quad Results}
We predict hourly demand at 118 bike-sharing stations with a gradient-boosted
model and compare it with a seasonal baseline under ten repetitions of
five-fold cross-validation.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Mean absolute error (bikes per hour) over ten repetitions of
five-fold cross-validation, lower is better.}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
Model & MAE & Stations \\
\midrule
Seasonal baseline & 4.82 & 118 \\
Boosted model     & 4.61 & 118 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The boosted model has a lower error than the baseline, but the difference is
not significant (corrected resampled $t$-test, $p = 0.41$).
Training takes 14 minutes on a laptop.
